How it works

Four checks, in this order.

Each step can stop the scan. A photo that fails a check is never turned into a diagnosis — it is sent back for a better shot.

  1. 01

    Take the photo

    Centre the leaf, keep it well lit, and hold steady. The camera only opens when you tap the button.

  2. 02

    Quality gate

    Brightness, exposure, focus and texture are measured from the actual pixels. Too dark or too blurry, and you are asked again.

  3. 03

    Crop gate

    Green walls and grass textures get rejected before any disease model runs. Only real foliage goes forward.

  4. 04

    Result and guidance

    You get the leading disease, the alternatives, how confident the model is, and what to do — treatment, prevention and safety.

Who it's for

Three roles, three levels of access.

Farmer

Scan crops, read the guidance, and keep a personal record of every result. You see your own data and nothing else.

  • Mobile-first scanning
  • Personal scan history
  • Daily, weekly and monthly trends

DA Officer

Watch disease pressure across the barangays inside one assigned municipality. Nothing outside that boundary.

  • Prevalence by barangay and crop
  • Hotspot detection
  • Jurisdiction-scoped records

Admin

Honest limits

What this tool does not claim.

It is not a diagnosis

AI output is a preliminary reading. Confirm with your municipal agriculturist before spending on treatment.

Only two crops

Tomato and palay. Other crops are rejected rather than guessed at.

Uncertain results are not saved as cases

If the model is not confident, you get a retry prompt — not a label on your record.

Your photos are not training data

Images stay in private storage. Nothing is used to train a model unless you explicitly opt in.

Start with one leaf.

Registration takes under a minute. All you need is your barangay.
